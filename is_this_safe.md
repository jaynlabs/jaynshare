# Is subscription sharing safe?

**Commercial sharing of personal Claude or ChatGPT/Codex subscriptions conflicts
with the providers' published rules.** A proxy, company ownership, or account-owner
consent does not authorize it.

Subscription risks only; cybersecurity is outside this note.
Reviewed: **16 September 2026**.

## What can happen?

- **Account suspension or termination:** enrolled accounts can lose access,
  including their owners' use outside Jaynshare.
- **Financial loss:** subscription value may be lost. Anthropic excludes refunds
  for termination due to violations, subject to applicable law.
- **Shared consequences:** another participant's activity can consume quota or
  trigger enforcement against the account used.
- **Service interruption:** bans, withdrawals, or provider changes can stop the
  service, leading to customer refund demands or claims.

Enforcement likelihood is unknown. Neither provider should be presented as a
safe option, and losses are not necessarily limited to subscription fees.

## Who bears the risk?

| Model | Main exposure |
| --- | --- |
| Company supplies subscriptions | Company accounts and spend; buyers' payments and service continuity. |
| Customers contribute subscriptions | Contributors' existing accounts, subscription spend, and personal usage. |
| Mixed pool | Both; disclose that unrelated contributors supply capacity. |

A buyer who never enrolls their own provider account is not directly putting
that account into the pool.

## What does consent change?

**Consent is not provider permission or a blanket liability waiver.** Explain
withdrawal, refunds, and any contributor compensation. Do not guarantee account
reinstatement or uninterrupted access.

An authorized API-backed service or a written provider agreement is a different
contractual route, with its own obligations.
